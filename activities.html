Things to Do in Kigali.

Top 10 Activities (Ordered List)

  1. Visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial

    A powerful memorial that honors the victims of the 1994 genocide. It helps you understand Rwanda's history.

  2. Explore Kimironko Market

    The largest market in Kigali where you can find food, clothes, and crafts.

  3. Hike Mount Kigali

    A nice hike that takes 2-3 hours. You can see the whole city from the top.

  4. Visit the Inema Arts Center

    A gallery showing works by Rwandan artists. You can also watch dance performances.

  5. Take a City Tour

    Join a guided tour to learn about Kigali's history and culture.

  6. Visit Nyamirambo Neighborhood

    One of Kigali's oldest neighborhoods with street art and local restaurants.

  7. Enjoy a Bike Tour

    Rent a bike and explore the city's clean streets.

  8. Visit the Kandt House Museum

    Rwanda's first natural history museum in the former residence of Richard Kandt.

  9. Shop at Craft Markets

    Buy traditional baskets and handmade crafts as souvenirs.

  10. Walk Around Kigali Heights

    A modern area with nice views, restaurants, and shops.
