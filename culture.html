About Rwandan Culture

Rwandan culture is rich in traditions, music, dance, and art. The country is known for traditional Intore dance, basketry (Agaseke), and drumming. Despite the tragic history of 1994, Rwanda has become a symbol of hope and unity.

Traditional Practices

  • Intore Dance

    A traditional warrior dance with music and storytelling. Watch performances at cultural centers.

  • Basket Weaving (Agaseke)

    Traditional peace baskets made by local women. You can learn to make them at cultural centers.

  • Drumming

    Traditional drums (Ingoma) were used for communication and ceremonies.

  • Coffee Culture

    Rwanda produces some of the world's best coffee. Visit coffee shops to try it.
