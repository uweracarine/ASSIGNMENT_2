Cultural Experiences in Kigali

About Rwandan Culture.

Rwandan culture is rich in traditions, music, dance, and art. The country is known for traditional Intore dance, basketry (Agaseke), and drumming. Despite the tragic history of 1994, Rwanda has become a symbol of hope and unity.

Museums and Memorials

Kigali Genocide Memorial

A memorial for the victims of the 1994 genocide. It is free to visit and open every day.

Location: Gisozi

Visit Website

Kandt House Museum

Rwanda's first natural history museum showing the country's wildlife and history.

Location: Nyamirambo

Entry: $5-10

Ethnographic Museum

Shows traditional Rwandan artifacts, royal items, and cultural objects.

Location: Huye (2.5 hours from Kigali)

Entry: $10

Traditional Practices

  • Intore Dance

    A traditional warrior dance with music and storytelling. You can watch performances at cultural centers.

  • Basket Weaving (Agaseke)

    Traditional peace baskets made by local women. You can learn to make them at cultural centers.

  • Drumming

    Traditional drums (Ingoma) were used for communication and ceremonies.

  • Coffee Culture

    Rwanda produces some of the world's best coffee. Visit coffee shops to try it.
