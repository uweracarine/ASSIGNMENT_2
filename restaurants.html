Places to Eat in Kigali.

Types of Restaurants (Unordered List)

  • Traditional Rwandan Restaurants

    Try local dishes like brochettes (grilled meat), ugali, and isombe.

  • International Restaurants

    Italian, Chinese, Indian, and French food available in Kigali.

  • Cafes and Coffee Shops

    Rwanda is famous for coffee. Visit cafes to try fresh Rwandan coffee.

  • Fast Food

    Pizza, burgers, and chicken restaurants are popular.

  • Street Food

    Cheap and tasty options like grilled corn and samosas.
