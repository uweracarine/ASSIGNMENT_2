About Kigali

Kigali is the capital and largest city of Rwanda. The city is located in the center of Rwanda and has a population of over 1 million people. Kigali is known for being clean, safe, and having friendly people.

There are many things to do in Kigali. You can visit museums, try local food, learn about Rwandan culture, and enjoy the nightlife. This website will help you explore the best of Kigali.
